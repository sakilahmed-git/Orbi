# Scintilla read-only verification report

## Executive summary

The core synthetic pipeline is substantially implemented and executable: the scene/event path, learned detector, synthetic disturbance estimator, envelope surrogate, control comparison, API, and the two intended data-backed frontend screens all have working code. However, the plan overstates reproducibility and real-data completion. e-STURT files now exist, but the real-data result remains qualitative only; several presentation numbers have no durable Section 4 artifact; and the landing dashboard is a separate client-only simulation rather than the backend pipeline (albeit its interactive telemetry and camera panels are labeled SIMULATION). On the present checkout, 2 sections are confirmed and 8 are partially confirmed; none of the core code files is missing or fails to import.

## Red flags

- Do not present any e-STURT quantitative validation: the actual available longest event sample is 1.535 s and the real-validation function returns `qualitative_format_check_only`.
- The landing dashboard is a standalone simulation, not a view of `api/main.py`. Its SIMULATION labels are an important safeguard; retain them. The adjacent static **MEASURED** card is potentially misleading because it looks like current backend telemetry.
- Correct the held-out classical aggregate wherever quoted: saved evidence says **47.6185 +/- 3.1693 px**, not **47.6498 +/- 3.2585 px**.
- Section 4's exact comparison claims have no extant plot/log source. The claimed PNG is absent.
- The saved classifier was serialized under sklearn 1.9.0 but executes under 1.8.0 with warnings; result reproducibility is not fully established.
- The worktree is not clean: `web/src/app/globals.css`, `web/src/components/MissionControl.tsx`, and `web/src/lib/useMissionSimulation.ts` are modified. Recent history is `113f74a chandu kumar`, then `b107dfc`, `be9f488`, etc.; this does not establish the plan's claimed final-integration state as a committed handoff.

## What's genuinely solid

- The synthetic data path is executable end-to-end today: scene generation, DVS conversion, detector, PSD estimate, control comparison, REST output, and streamed event/frame messages all ran successfully.
- The held-out scenario seeds are genuinely disjoint from the two training seeds, and the recorded learned mean error (**5.8855951869 px**) is directly traceable to `outputs/logs/section5_heldout_generalization.json`.
- The Section 6 non-round audit is traceable to `outputs/logs/section6_nonround_frequency_audit.json`, and source inspection confirms injected ground truth is not passed into the estimator.
- The Section 8 20/20 RMS-win and 11.7% improvement claims are directly supported by `outputs/logs/section8_control_loop_metrics.json`.
- All eight JSON logs in `outputs/logs/` are referenced by the build plan: Sections 5 (two), 6 (two), 7 (three), and 8 (one). No unmentioned/orphan JSON log was found.
- There were no TODO/FIXME/stub markers in source/package manifests. The only packaging issue observed is the non-fatal dual-lockfile Turbopack warning.
